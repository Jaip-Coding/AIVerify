HOW IT WORKS

Evidence first. Rules second. AI last.

01

Collect evidence

Fetch a public webpage, detect relevant technology and visible disclosures, and preserve what was actually observed.

02

Confirm facts

Ask the company about internal AI use, provider/deployer status and activities that cannot be inferred externally.

03

Apply rules

A deterministic rules engine maps evidence and declarations to Article 50 readiness checks. No LLM decides compliance.

04

Show uncertainty

Every result is Pass, Potential review, Requires confirmation or Not applicable, with confidence and evidence.

METHODOLOGY

Designed not to overclaim.

The product intentionally refuses to turn missing evidence into a legal conclusion. A chat widget indicator, for example, is not treated as proof that AI is used. Where the implementation cannot be verified, the result remains unknown or is flagged for review.

01No “compliant” badgeReadiness findings only.
